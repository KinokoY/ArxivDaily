### UniRAP: Towards Unified Part-level Physical Affordance Reasoning and Actionable Perception

**中文标题：** UniRAP：迈向统一的部件级物理可供性推理与可操作感知

**摘要：** Vision-language perception has achieved impressive progress in aligning natural language with visual observations, yet grounding high-level semantics into part-level physical interaction remains challenging. To address this gap, we propose UniRAP, a unified model for inferring part-level physical affordances and mapping language instructions to actionable geometric representations. UniRAP formulates this problem as a conditional multimodal generation task, integrating visual inputs, textual instructions, and optional prompts into a shared spatiotemporal representation space through a unified interface token mechanism. To predict executable contact geometry, we introduce a Unified Affordance Decoder (UAD), which jointly performs object detection, part-level affordance segmentation, and 4-DoF interaction pose estimation by leveraging intermediate segmentation features. In addition, we propose a curriculum-based transfer training strategy that progressively adapts the model from general visual parsing to interaction-aware perception, improving data efficiency under limited textual annotations. Experiments show that UniRAP achieves state-of-the-art performance on referring expression segmentation, affordance grounding, and interaction pose estimation, while maintaining strong spatiotemporal consistency in dynamic video scenarios. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of UniRAP as a unified perception framework for language-guided physical manipulation.

**摘要翻译：** 视觉-语言感知在将自然语言与视觉观察对齐方面已取得显著进展，但将高层语义落地为部件级物理交互仍然具有挑战性。为填补这一空白，我们提出 UniRAP，一个用于推断部件级物理可供性并将语言指令映射为可操作几何表示的统一模型。UniRAP 将该问题建模为条件多模态生成任务，通过统一的接口令牌机制将视觉输入、文本指令和可选提示整合到共享的时空表示空间中。为预测可执行的接触几何，我们引入统一可供性解码器（UAD），其通过利用中间分割特征联合执行目标检测、部件级可供性分割和 4-DoF 交互姿态估计。此外，我们提出一种基于课程的迁移训练策略，逐步将模型从通用视觉解析适配到交互感知感知，从而在文本标注有限的情况下提升数据效率。实验表明，UniRAP 在指代表达分割、可供性定位和交互姿态估计上取得了最先进的性能，同时在动态视频场景中保持较强的时空一致性。这些结果证明了 UniRAP 作为语言引导物理操作统一感知框架的有效性。

### Complexity-Aware Surgical Video Reasoning Segmentation with Digital Twin and Context Memory

**中文标题：** 复杂度感知的手术视频推理分割：结合数字孪生与上下文记忆

**摘要：** Conventional surgical segmentation relies on closed-set categories or explicit text queries, which cannot resolve implicit queries that require reasoning about workflows, instrument functions, and temporal relationships. To address these limitations, we propose SurgReasonSeg, a complexity-aware method that brings reasoning segmentation over digital twin (DT) representations to surgical video. Specifically, SurgReasonSeg constructs DT representations from surgical foundation models and trains two low-rank adapters (LoRA) of one large language model with reinforcement learning (RL), each specialized for a different query complexity. A lightweight router, also trained with RL, selects between the two adapters to balance latency against accuracy. SurgReasonSeg also sets the temporal context with an adaptive sliding window conditioned on query complexity and workflow phase, and retrieves relevant historical cases from a surgical context memory by workflow similarity and procedural relevance. We further introduce EndoVis-ReasonSeg, a surgical reasoning segmentation benchmark with 1,460 training and 961 test samples across semantic, spatial, and temporal categories. On EndoVis-ReasonSeg, SurgReasonSeg improves J&F over the strongest baseline, ReSurgSAM2, by 0.178, 0.237, and 0.297 on semantic, spatial, and temporal queries, and it also achieves the best results on the Ref-EndoVis17 and Ref-EndoVis18 referring segmentation benchmarks. A backbone-matched attribution protocol separates the contribution of the upstream segmenter from that of the reasoning modules. Our method runs at 18.8 FPS averaged over the query distribution, enabling real-time reasoning segmentation in surgical videos.

**摘要翻译：** 传统手术分割依赖闭集类别或显式文本查询，无法解决需要对工作流程、器械功能和时间关系进行推理的隐式查询。为解决这些局限，我们提出SurgReasonSeg，一种复杂度感知方法，将基于数字孪生（DT）表示的推理分割引入手术视频。具体而言，SurgReasonSeg从手术基础模型构建DT表示，并用强化学习（RL）训练一个大语言模型的两个低秩适配器（LoRA），每个适配器针对不同的查询复杂度专门化。一个同样使用RL训练的轻量级路由器在两个适配器之间进行选择，以平衡延迟与准确率。SurgReasonSeg还根据查询复杂度与工作流程阶段设置自适应滑动窗口的时间上下文，并依据工作流程相似性与程序相关性从手术上下文记忆中检索相关历史病例。我们进一步引入EndoVis-ReasonSeg，一个手术推理分割基准，包含跨语义、空间和时间类别的1,460个训练样本和961个测试样本。在EndoVis-ReasonSeg上，SurgReasonSeg在语义、空间和时间查询上分别将J&F较最强基线ReSurgSAM2提升0.178、0.237和0.297，并在Ref-EndoVis17和Ref-EndoVis18指代分割基准上取得最佳结果。一种骨干匹配的归因协议将上游分割器与推理模块的贡献区分开来。我们的方法在查询分布上平均以18.8 FPS运行，实现了手术视频中的实时推理分割。
