### RepConTUR: A Report-Conditioned Volumetric Multimodal Model for Abdominal Tumor Segmentation

**中文标题：** RepConTUR：一种用于腹部肿瘤分割的报告条件体积多模态模型

**摘要：** Tumor delineation is required for radiotherapy planning, yet automatic segmentation is limited by errors, predominantly false positives, that distort dose and increase editing time. The radiology report accompanies the scan and localizes disease at the suborgan level, but existing text-promptable models condition on short referring phrases, and most operate in 2D. We present RepConTUR, a single native-3D early-fusion multimodal model that conditions volumetric abdominal tumor segmentation on the narrative report. We extend AbdomenAtlas 3.0 to a suborgan-level promptable segmentation dataset and generate report-based VQA pairs, yielding four report-derived tasks on the same annotations: referring segmentation (RES), report-grounded segmentation (GSEG), report generation (RRG), and visual question answering (VQA). We train RepConTUR, comprising a 3D CT encoder, an early-fusion language model, and a native-3D segmentation module, jointly on the four tasks. Across liver, kidney and pancreas, RepConTUR attains the highest Dice similarity coefficient (DSC), instance-wise precision, and detection F1 among the text-promptable baselines, and adding the report raises mean precision while leaving recall comparable. Against two vision-only specialists, nnU-Net and the report-supervised R-Super, RepConTUR attains the highest value on every in-domain metric for liver and pancreas and the highest mean DSC, recall, precision, and detection F1 over the four out-of-distribution public cohorts. Ablations indicate that conditioning on the narrative report raises detection F1 from 0.499 to 0.528 within the same model, that joint GSEG training raises RES F1 from 0.450 to 0.482, and that the native-3D segmentation module raises GSEG F1 from 0.139 to 0.528 over its 2D counterpart. Code is available at https://github.com/Oncosoft-Research/RepConTUR.

**摘要翻译：** 肿瘤勾画是放疗计划所必需的，但自动分割受限于误差，主要是假阳性，这些误差会扭曲剂量并增加编辑时间。影像报告随扫描一同提供，并在亚器官层面定位疾病，但现有的文本提示模型以简短的指代短语为条件，且大多在2D中运行。我们提出RepConTUR，一种单一的原生3D早期融合多模态模型，以叙述性报告为条件进行体积腹部肿瘤分割。我们将AbdomenAtlas 3.0扩展为亚器官级可提示分割数据集，并生成基于报告的VQA对，在同一标注上产生四个源自报告的任务：指代分割（RES）、报告接地分割（GSEG）、报告生成（RRG）和视觉问答（VQA）。我们训练RepConTUR，其由3D CT编码器、早期融合语言模型和原生3D分割模块组成，在四个任务上联合训练。在肝脏、肾脏和胰腺上，RepConTUR在文本提示基线中取得了最高的Dice相似系数（DSC）、实例级精确率和检测F1，并且添加报告提高了平均精确率，同时召回率保持相当。与两个仅视觉的专家模型nnU-Net和报告监督的R-Super相比，RepConTUR在肝脏和胰腺的每个域内指标上取得最高值，并在四个分布外公共队列上取得最高的平均DSC、召回率、精确率和检测F1。消融实验表明，以叙述性报告为条件在同一模型内将检测F1从0.499提高到0.528，联合GSEG训练将RES F1从0.450提高到0.482，并且原生3D分割模块相比其2D对应物将GSEG F1从0.139提高到0.528。代码可在https://github.com/Oncosoft-Research/RepConTUR获取。

### Event-SAMA: Language-Grounded Video Object Segmentation on Neuromorphic Event Streams

**中文标题：** Event-SAMA：基于神经形态事件流的语言接地视频目标分割

**摘要：** Event cameras offer microsecond temporal resolution, high dynamic range and no motion blur, but referring video object segmentation (RVOS) lacks event-stream datasets and models. We introduce Event-SAMA-239K, a synthetic-event conversion of the SAMA-239K grounded video-chat corpus and all additional SAMA training data: 30,650 video clips and 392,162 images. Optical-flow, FILM or saccade upsampling followed by a linear event generator preserves all mask, referring-expression and dialogue annotations. We also introduce Event-SAMA, adapting SAMA-4B with a two-channel polarity patch embedding initialised from pretrained RGB weights and a zero-initialised event adapter. End-to-end training yields 24.37 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$ on MeViS valid_u and 22.33 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$ on the converted ReVOS valid subset, establishing the first event-native RVOS baseline (RGB SAMA-4B: 55.4 on MeViS). Frozen RGB SAMA-4B with pseudo-RGB renderings or E2VID reconstructions of the same events scores 13.23 and 15.94 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$. Without retraining, Event-SAMA works on real event-camera recordings when the accumulation window matches their event density. A seed-controlled ablation with a measured noise floor of 0.47 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$ finds gains from diverse training sources over MeViS alone (+4.99), RGB-mean event-stem initialisation (+4.33), and a trainable SAM2 mask decoder (+2.76); the event adapter, partial ViT unfreezing and event polarity are within noise at this budget. An earlier, partially converted corpus snapshot trained with the identical recipe scores 1.38 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$ higher on MeViS and 1.12 higher on ReVOS. Both are single runs that also differ in schedule length, so we draw no conclusion about corpus scale.

**摘要翻译：** 事件相机具有微秒级时间分辨率、高动态范围和无运动模糊的特性，但指代视频目标分割（RVOS）缺乏事件流数据集和模型。我们提出了 Event-SAMA-239K，这是对 SAMA-239K 接地视频对话语料库及所有额外 SAMA 训练数据的合成事件转换：包含 30,650 个视频片段和 392,162 张图像。通过光流、FILM 或眼跳上采样后接线性事件生成器，保留了所有掩码、指代表达和对话标注。我们还提出了 Event-SAMA，通过从预训练 RGB 权重初始化的双通道极性块嵌入和零初始化事件适配器对 SAMA-4B 进行适配。端到端训练在 MeViS valid_u 上得到 24.37 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$，在转换后的 ReVOS 验证子集上得到 22.33 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$，建立了首个事件原生 RVOS 基线（RGB SAMA-4B 在 MeViS 上为 55.4）。冻结的 RGB SAMA-4B 使用相同事件的伪 RGB 渲染或 E2VID 重建，分别得到 13.23 和 15.94 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$。无需重新训练，当累积窗口与真实事件相机记录的事件密度匹配时，Event-SAMA 可在真实事件相机记录上工作。一项种子控制的消融实验（测得噪声底为 0.47 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$）发现：相比仅使用 MeViS，多样化训练来源带来增益（+4.99），RGB 均值事件干初始化带来增益（+4.33），可训练的 SAM2 掩码解码器带来增益（+2.76）；在此预算下，事件适配器、部分 ViT 解冻和事件极性处于噪声范围内。一个较早的、部分转换的语料库快照使用相同配方训练，在 MeViS 上得分高 1.38 $\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}$，在 ReVOS 上高 1.12。两者均为单次运行且调度长度不同，因此我们不对语料库规模得出结论。

### CASE: Concept-Aware Segmentation from Positive and Negative Visual Exemplars

**中文标题：** CASE：基于正负视觉示例的概念感知分割

**摘要：** Few-shot semantic segmentation typically identifies target objects based on positive support examples of a single semantic class. In practice, however, positive cues alone are often underspecified: for instance, "red cars" may be confused with cars of all colors, "vehicles" may include cars that should be excluded, and "material-defined objects" may span multiple categories. Fulfilling precise requests requires positive exemplars to provide inclusion evidence and negative exemplars to act as intent-defining constraints for exclusion and disambiguation. We formulate this setting as Specified Visual Prompt Segmentation (SVPS), where the target concept is specified by both positive and negative visual prompts. In this work, visual exemplars refer to support-image regions specified by bounding-box prompts. To study SVPS, we introduce CASE (Concept-Aware Segmentation from Positive and Negative Visual Exemplars), a benchmark built on COCO and PACO. CASE encompasses six category- and attribute-level task families: exclusion, isolation, union, attribute exclusion, attribute isolation, and attribute union. We evaluate SAM 3 under this setting and find that, despite its powerful positive-only few-shot segmentation capability, frozen SAM 3 does not consistently treat negative visual prompts as exclusion evidence. To address this, we present Concept Prompt Adaptation (CPA), a conservative LoRA-based adaptation baseline that improves positive-negative prompt interpretation on CASE while remaining competitive under positive-only few-shot segmentation. Code is available at https://github.com/WongKinYiu/FSS-CASE.

**摘要翻译：** 少样本语义分割通常基于单一语义类别的正支持示例来识别目标对象。然而在实践中，仅靠正线索往往不够明确：例如，“红色汽车”可能会与各种颜色的汽车混淆，“车辆”可能会包含本应排除的汽车，而“由材质定义的对象”可能跨越多个类别。满足精确请求需要正示例提供包含证据，负示例作为意图定义约束以用于排除和消歧。我们将这一设定表述为指定视觉提示分割（Specified Visual Prompt Segmentation, SVPS），其中目标概念由正负视觉提示共同指定。在本工作中，视觉示例指由边界框提示指定的支持图像区域。为研究 SVPS，我们引入了 CASE（Concept-Aware Segmentation from Positive and Negative Visual Exemplars），一个基于 COCO 和 PACO 构建的基准。CASE 涵盖六个类别级和属性级任务族：排除、隔离、并集、属性排除、属性隔离和属性并集。我们在该设定下评估 SAM 3，发现尽管其具有强大的仅正示例少样本分割能力，冻结的 SAM 3 并不能始终将负视觉提示视为排除证据。为解决这一问题，我们提出了概念提示适配（Concept Prompt Adaptation, CPA），一种保守的基于 LoRA 的适配基线，它在 CASE 上改进了正负提示解释，同时在仅正示例少样本分割下保持竞争力。代码见 https://github.com/WongKinYiu/FSS-CASE。
