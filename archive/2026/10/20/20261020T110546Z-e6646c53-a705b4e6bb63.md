### ATLAS: Learning to Adapt LVLM Attention for Reasoning Video Segmentation

**中文标题：** ATLAS：学习适配 LVLM 注意力以实现推理视频分割

**摘要：** Reasoning Video Object Segmentation (RVOS) aims to localize and segment objects referred to by natural-language expressions throughout a video. Although large vision-language models (LVLMs) implicitly encode localization cues in their attention maps, these attentions are often noisy, diffuse, and scattered across layers, heads, and modalities, limiting their direct use for segmentation. Existing methods typically rely on handcrafted attention-selection heuristics or computationally expensive attention refinement. We propose ATLAS, an attention adaptation framework that learns to transform raw LVLM attentions into object-centric localization cues. ATLAS treats attentions from all layers, heads, and modalities as structured features, combining high-resolution frame-level attentions that capture spatial information with low-resolution video-level attentions that encode temporal context. A lightweight U-Net equipped with temporal attention and FiLM-based modulation fuses these complementary representations to generate coarse segmentation heatmaps, which are propagated across the video using SAM2. Extensive experiments on challenging referring and reasoning video segmentation benchmarks show that ATLAS outperforms existing frozen-backbone methods. Code and models are available at \url{https://hamidreza-136.github.io/atlas/}.

**摘要翻译：** 推理视频目标分割（RVOS）旨在定位并分割视频中由自然语言表达所指代的对象。尽管大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）在其注意力图中隐式编码了定位线索，但这些注意力往往噪声大、分散，并散落在不同层、头和模态之间，限制了它们直接用于分割。现有方法通常依赖手工设计的注意力选择启发式规则或计算昂贵的注意力细化。我们提出 ATLAS，一个注意力适配框架，学习将原始 LVLM 注意力转换为以对象为中心的定位线索。ATLAS 将所有层、头和模态的注意力视为结构化特征，将捕获空间信息的高分辨率帧级注意力与编码时间上下文的低分辨率视频级注意力相结合。一个配备时间注意力和基于 FiLM 的调制的轻量级 U-Net 融合这些互补表示以生成粗分割热图，并通过 SAM2 在视频中传播。在具有挑战性的指代和推理视频分割基准上的大量实验表明，ATLAS 优于现有的冻结骨干方法。代码和模型可在 \url{https://hamidreza-136.github.io/atlas/} 获取。

**来源：** [arXiv 2610.24783v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24783v1)

### GeoReason: Bridging Logical Reasoning and Spatial Fidelity in Remote Sensing Segmentation

**中文标题：** GeoReason：弥合遥感分割中的逻辑推理与空间保真度

**摘要：** Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have significantly advanced instruction-driven perception through the "Embedding-as-Mask" paradigm. However, extending this capability to Remote Sensing (RS) reasoning segmentation remains exceptionally challenging under cluttered geographical contexts and extreme scale variations, often leading to Spatial Fidelity Degradation and Target Misidentification. We identify a critical yet underexplored bottleneck: the sequential alienation of the segmentation token [SEG] from its initial visual grounding within the autoregressive generation paradigm. Produced only at the end of long reasoning chains, [SEG] relies on visual evidence that has been progressively diluted by linguistic abstraction and autoregressive noise. This information decay is particularly catastrophic for RS targets with minuscule pixel footprints, where even marginal fidelity loss leads to localization failure. To address these bottlenecks, we propose GeoReason, a novel framework designed to fortify logical deduction and preserve spatial integrity. First, we introduce an Anticipatory Prior mechanism that injects the [SEG] token directly into the initial user query, shifting it from a terminal output to a primary condition. This paradigm shift transforms the token into a persistent spatial anchor, preventing information decay during long-form linguistic inference. Second, we enhance visual reasoning via a Location-aware Chain-of-Thought (CoT) that enforces coarse-to-fine localization using bounding boxes, coupled with a Latent Patch-to-Token Alignment (PTA) Loss that explicitly aligns the latent segmentation token with physical RS textures. Extensive experiments demonstrate that GeoReason consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods on the Earthreason and RRSIS-D benchmarks. Code is available at https://github.com/Fanc0126/GeoReason.

**摘要翻译：** 多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）通过“嵌入即掩码”范式显著推进了指令驱动的感知能力。然而，在杂乱的地理语境和极端尺度变化下，将该能力扩展到遥感（RS）推理分割仍然异常困难，往往导致空间保真度退化与目标误识别。我们识别出一个关键但尚未被充分探索的瓶颈：在自回归生成范式中，分割标记[SEG]与其初始视觉 grounding 的顺序性疏离。由于[SEG]仅在长推理链末尾产生，它依赖的视觉证据已被语言抽象和自回归噪声逐步稀释。这种信息衰减对于像素足迹极小的遥感目标尤为灾难性，即使微小的保真度损失也会导致定位失败。为解决这些瓶颈，我们提出GeoReason，一种旨在强化逻辑推演并保持空间完整性的新框架。首先，我们引入预判先验机制，将[SEG]标记直接注入初始用户查询中，使其从终端输出转变为首要条件。这一范式转变将该标记转化为持续的空间锚点，防止长形式语言推理过程中的信息衰减。其次，我们通过位置感知思维链（CoT）增强视觉推理，该思维链利用边界框强制实现由粗到细的定位，并结合潜在块到标记对齐（PTA）损失，显式地将潜在分割标记与物理遥感纹理对齐。大量实验表明，GeoReason在Earthreason和RRSIS-D基准上始终优于最先进的方法。代码可在https://github.com/Fanc0126/GeoReason获取。

**来源：** [arXiv 2610.23416v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23416v1)

### Xiaomi-Lens-0 Technical Report

**中文标题：** 小米-Lens-0 技术报告

**摘要：** Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) answer questions about whole images well, yet they still struggle with fine-grained region understanding. A key reason is that text-only supervision never specifies which regions support an answer. To close this gap, we present Xiaomi-Lens-0, a 4B MLLM built on Qwen3.5 that predicts masks from its own visual representations. Specifically, a lightweight decoder combines vision-encoder patch features with the language model's mask-token states, termed visual latents, so that mask supervision reaches the shared backbone without an additional segmentation encoder. Because each latent decodes to a mask, the model can interleave reasoning with region-specific latents and thus think with visual latents, while grounded reasoning traces supervise the intermediate regions referenced during chain-of-thought. As a result, across all eight RefCOCO, RefCOCO+, and RefCOCOg splits, Xiaomi-Lens-0 gains 5.6 points on average over Qwen3.5 in referring comprehension and achieves the highest segmentation cIoU among compared models. Moreover, it surpasses Qwen3.5 across all perception benchmarks.
  Homepage: https://seerray-lab.github.io/Xiaomi-Lens-0

**摘要翻译：** 多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）能很好地回答关于整张图像的问题，但在细粒度区域理解上仍存在困难。一个关键原因是，纯文本监督从未指明哪些区域支持某个答案。为弥补这一差距，我们提出 Xiaomi-Lens-0，一个基于 Qwen3.5 构建的 4B MLLM，它从自身的视觉表示中预测掩码。具体而言，一个轻量级解码器将视觉编码器的 patch 特征与语言模型的掩码 token 状态（称为视觉潜变量）相结合，使得掩码监督能够到达共享主干，而无需额外的分割编码器。由于每个潜变量都可解码为一个掩码，模型可以将推理与区域特定的潜变量交错进行，从而以视觉潜变量进行思考，同时基于区域的推理轨迹监督思维链过程中引用的中间区域。结果，在全部八个 RefCOCO、RefCOCO+ 和 RefCOCOg 划分上，Xiaomi-Lens-0 在指代理解上比 Qwen3.5 平均提升 5.6 分，并在所比较的模型中取得了最高的分割 cIoU。此外，它在所有感知基准上都超过了 Qwen3.5。
  主页：https://seerray-lab.github.io/Xiaomi-Lens-0
