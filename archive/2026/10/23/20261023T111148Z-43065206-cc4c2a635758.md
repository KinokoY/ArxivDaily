### LLMSeg: Training-Free Text-Prompted Image Segmentation with B-Splines

**中文标题：** LLMSeg：基于B样条的无训练文本提示图像分割

**摘要：** Promptable segmentation models such as SAM 3 achieve remarkable open-vocabulary performance but require dedicated architectures, large-scale annotation engines, and task-specific training. We ask a different question: can a general-purpose multimodal large language model (MLLM) segment images directly, without an added segmentation head, mask decoder, or training? We present LLMSeg, a training-free pipeline that turns a vision-capable LLM into a text-prompted image segmenter. LLMSeg decomposes segmentation into three LLM-driven stages. First, detect localizes all instances matching a free-form text query and describes their coarse morphology. Next, segment traces each instance using closed periodic B-splines confined to its detected box. These splines define an outer boundary and optional hole loops. Finally, refine iteratively proposes focus boxes around residual errors and retraces confined splines. The process ends when the mask stabilizes or the pass budget is exhausted. The output is a binary mask with inspectable intermediate geometry. We evaluate LLMSeg across ten referring and reasoning segmentation splits (ReasonSeg and RefCOCO/+/g). We also evaluate open-vocabulary instance segmentation (LVIS, COCO, and SA-Co), semantic segmentation, and zero-shot detection (ODinW13 and RF-100VL). With an off-the-shelf Gemini-3.7-Flash backend, LLMSeg is competitive with and often surpasses the specialist SAM 3 model on identical items. It reaches 71.5 cIoU on ReasonSeg val without additional segmentation-specific training. Backend model updates can improve accuracy without retraining the pipeline. The code is available at https://abdullahamdi.com/llmseg .

**摘要翻译：** SAM 3 等可提示分割模型实现了出色的开放词汇性能，但需要专用架构、大规模标注引擎以及任务特定训练。我们提出一个不同的问题：通用多模态大语言模型（MLLM）能否在没有额外分割头、掩码解码器或训练的情况下直接分割图像？我们提出 LLMSeg，一个无需训练的流程，将具备视觉能力的 LLM 转变为文本提示图像分割器。LLMSeg 将分割分解为三个由 LLM 驱动的阶段。首先，detect 定位与自由形式文本查询匹配的所有实例，并描述其粗略形态。接着，segment 使用限制在检测框内的闭合周期 B 样条追踪每个实例。这些样条定义外部边界和可选的孔洞环。最后，refine 迭代地在残差误差周围提出聚焦框，并重新追踪受限样条。当掩码稳定或遍历预算耗尽时，该过程结束。输出是带有可检查中间几何的二进制掩码。我们在十个指代与推理分割划分（ReasonSeg 和 RefCOCO/+/g）上评估 LLMSeg。我们还评估开放词汇实例分割（LVIS、COCO 和 SA-Co）、语义分割和零样本检测（ODinW13 和 RF-100VL）。使用现成的 Gemini-3.7-Flash 后端，LLMSeg 在相同项目上与专用 SAM 3 模型具有竞争力，且常常超越后者。它在 ReasonSeg val 上达到 71.5 cIoU，无需额外的分割特定训练。后端模型更新可以在不重新训练流程的情况下提高准确率。代码可在 https://abdullahamdi.com/llmseg 获取。

### OccJEPA: Occlusion as Masking for Amodal Video Segmentation

**中文标题：** OccJEPA：将遮挡视为掩码的完整视频分割

**摘要：** Amodal video segmentation asks for the complete mask of an object in every frame, including the parts hidden behind other objects. The best current methods generate these masks with fine-tuned video diffusion models, which need many denoising steps and an extra depth network, and give a different answer for every sample. Self-supervised video world models of the JEPA family are trained on a closely related task: predicting the representation of masked space-time regions from the visible ones. We therefore treat occlusion as masking. The tokens that the object's visible mask does not cover are dropped, the pre-trained V-JEPA 2.1 predictor fills them in, and a light head decodes the observed and predicted features into amodal masks. The resulting model, OccJEPA (Occlusion-as-masking JEPA), is deterministic and 33.5x faster than the diffusion-based state of the art, and more accurate on SAIL-VOS, on TAO-Amodal, including a held-out test split (+4.00 AP50) and visible masks tracked by SAM2, and on Kubric. Controlled experiments trace the gain to the predictor's pre-trained weights rather than to its capacity, for world models from 87M to 1.85B parameters. Code is available at https://github.com/zgp110/occjepa.

**摘要翻译：** 完整视频分割要求给出每一帧中物体的完整掩码，包括被其他物体遮挡的部分。当前最佳方法使用微调的视频扩散模型生成这些掩码，但需要大量去噪步骤和额外的深度网络，且每次采样都会给出不同的答案。JEPA 系列的自监督视频世界模型在一个密切相关的任务上训练：从可见区域预测被掩码的时空区域的表示。因此，我们将遮挡视为掩码。物体可见掩码未覆盖的 token 被丢弃，预训练的 V-JEPA 2.1 预测器将其填补，一个轻量头部将观测到的特征和预测的特征解码为完整掩码。由此得到的模型 OccJEPA（Occlusion-as-masking JEPA）是确定性的，比基于扩散的最先进方法快 33.5 倍，并且在 SAIL-VOS、TAO-Amodal（包括一个留出测试划分，+4.00 AP50，以及由 SAM2 跟踪的可见掩码）和 Kubric 上更准确。在 87M 到 1.85B 参数的世界模型上进行的控制实验表明，增益来自预测器的预训练权重，而非其容量。代码见 https://github.com/zgp110/occjepa。

**来源：** [arXiv 2610.29061v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29061v1)
