### RSReasoner: Reformulating and Auditing Geospatial Reasoning Segmentation

**中文标题：** RSReasoner：重新表述与审计地理空间推理分割

**摘要：** Geospatial reasoning segmentation aims to localize geographic targets from language requiring semantic inference beyond direct visual perception. However, existing benchmarks often operationalize reasoning as category omission while overlooking attribute mixing, which neglects explicit-category reasoning, introduces perceptual shortcuts, and fails to precisely evaluate reasoning capability. To diagnose and address these limitations, we first reformulate geospatial reasoning segmentation along category expression (explicit vs. implicit) and attribute nature (perceptual vs. inferential), and audit 90,708 queries across existing benchmarks to reveal that pure explicit-inferential queries account for only 0.61% whereas 31.98% mix both attribute types. We then develop a three-stage pipeline that harnesses OpenStreetMap attributes while mitigating geometric and semantic noise to construct RSReasoner, a large-scale dataset of 106,610 images with 213,220 reasoning queries, and RSReasoner-Bench, a diagnostic benchmark of 75,400 queries across four canonical types and controlled variants. Extensive experiments demonstrate that RSReasoner-Bench precisely evaluates model capability across multiple dimensions while exposing shortcut behaviors, and fine-tuning on RSReasoner boosts both reasoning and cross-type generalization. Our findings offer key insights for future geospatial reasoning research and benchmark design. Datasets and code will be released at https://github.com/Ricardo-XZ/RSReasoner.

**摘要翻译：** 地理空间推理分割旨在根据需要超越直接视觉感知的语义推理的语言来定位地理目标。然而，现有基准往往将推理操作化为类别省略，同时忽视属性混合，这忽略了显式类别推理，引入了感知捷径，并且无法精确评估推理能力。为了诊断并解决这些局限，我们首先沿类别表达（显式与隐式）和属性性质（感知与推理性）重新表述地理空间推理分割，并审计现有基准中的90,708条查询，揭示纯显式-推理性查询仅占0.61%，而31.98%混合了两种属性类型。随后，我们开发了一个三阶段流程，利用OpenStreetMap属性同时缓解几何与语义噪声，构建了RSReasoner——一个包含106,610张图像和213,220条推理查询的大规模数据集，以及RSReasoner-Bench——一个包含75,400条查询、覆盖四种典型类型和受控变体的诊断基准。大量实验表明，RSReasoner-Bench在多个维度上精确评估模型能力，同时暴露捷径行为，而在RSReasoner上微调可提升推理能力和跨类型泛化能力。我们的发现为未来地理空间推理研究和基准设计提供了关键见解。数据集和代码将在https://github.com/Ricardo-XZ/RSReasoner发布。

### Visual Thinking: Training-Free Active Perception for Counting and Localizing Hard-to-Find Objects

**中文标题：** 视觉思考：面向难以发现目标的计数与定位的无训练主动感知

**摘要：** Vision foundation models can precisely segment objects based solely on a text prompt. However, they struggle to recover objects that are small, occluded or whose appearance is poorly captured by the original textual description. Vision language models can examine the image and its context, they can propose richer descriptions of the objects to be detected. But these models struggle with precise segmentation and localization. We introduce Visual Thinking, a training-free framework that combines a vision-language model with Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3) through an iterative active perception loop. At each iteration, the VLM proposes short noun phrases using the image, candidate crops and history from past iterations. A controller ranks these phrases using embedding similarity between the original prompt and the proposal, an entropy-based verification priority and the novelty of the prompt. Next, SAM 3 uses prompts to detect objects in the image. We associate detections across iterations and update each object's confidence score using a weighted mean of scores obtained from SAM 3 with a larger weight for detections using narrower prompts. We supply confident detections as positive visual exemplars for SAM 3, and incorporate image tiling to recover small objects in cluttered images. We evaluate counting and localization on CARPK, FSCD-147, PixMo-Count, and OmniCount (Fruits). Compared with SAM 3, Visual Thinking reduces the counting mean absolute error (MAE) by about 18 on FSC-147 and about 8 on CARPK. It improves box average precision (AP) by more than 12 on FSCD-147 and CARPK. Visual Thinking, with fixed or no tiling, performs on par with Claude Opus 5.5 at extra-high reasoning effort, while running on single desktop GPU in about 5.91 seconds per image, compared to about 46.53 seconds per image for Claude through its API.

**摘要翻译：** 视觉基础模型能够仅依据文本提示精确分割目标。然而，对于小目标、被遮挡目标，或外观无法被原始文本描述充分捕捉的目标，它们难以将其找回。视觉语言模型能够审视图像及其上下文，可以提出对待检测目标更丰富的描述。但这些模型在精确分割和定位方面表现不佳。我们提出视觉思考（Visual Thinking），一个无需训练的框架，通过迭代式主动感知循环将视觉语言模型与 Segment Anything Model 3（SAM 3）相结合。在每次迭代中，VLM 利用图像、候选裁剪区域以及以往迭代的历史，提出简短的名词短语。控制器依据原始提示与所提短语之间的嵌入相似度、基于熵的验证优先级以及提示的新颖性，对这些短语进行排序。随后，SAM 3 使用提示在图像中检测目标。我们在各次迭代之间关联检测结果，并使用从 SAM 3 获得的分数的加权均值更新每个目标的置信度分数，其中对使用更窄提示的检测赋予更大权重。我们将高置信度的检测结果作为正视觉示例提供给 SAM 3，并引入图像分块以找回杂乱图像中的小目标。我们在 CARPK、FSCD-147、PixMo-Count 和 OmniCount（Fruits）上评估计数与定位。与 SAM 3 相比，Visual Thinking 在 FSC-147 上将计数平均绝对误差（MAE）降低约 18，在 CARPK 上降低约 8。它在 FSCD-147 和 CARPK 上将框平均精度（AP）提升超过 12。Visual Thinking 在固定分块或无分块的情况下，性能与以超高推理强度运行的 Claude Opus 5.5 相当，同时在单块桌面 GPU 上每张图像运行约 5.91 秒，而 Claude 通过其 API 每张图像约 46.53 秒。

### SpatialCraft: Evolving Procedural Intelligence from Experience and Skills for Spatial Reasoning Agents

**中文标题：** SpatialCraft: 从经验与技能演化程序化智能以实现空间推理智能体

**摘要：** Spatial reasoning agents increasingly rely on external tools, such as 3D reconstruction, segmentation, and geometric computation, to answer questions that a multimodal large language model (MLLM) cannot resolve from pixels alone. Tool access, however, shifts the bottleneck to orchestration: the agent must decide which tools to call, in what order, and how to interpret their outputs, yet a stateless agent repeats the same mistakes across tasks. We ask whether a frozen tool-using agent can improve its orchestration from its own interaction history, without parameter updates. We present SpatialCraft, a self-evolving framework that maintains two forms of external knowledge: Experiences, condition-action tactics distilled by contrasting rollouts of the same task, and Skills, multi-step procedures with explicit activation and termination conditions. Three designs couple the two streams. Experiences can be anchored to a Skill, appearing only while it is active rather than once per task. A Contrastive Utility Score estimates each Experience's effect by randomly withholding it across rollouts of the same task, separating its contribution from task difficulty, and Experiences with consistently positive utility are consolidated into the Skills they support. Across five spatial benchmarks and four backbones, SpatialCraft achieves the best accuracy in all 20 settings, showing that a frozen agent can learn to orchestrate spatial tools from its own experience.

**摘要翻译：** 空间推理智能体日益依赖外部工具（如3D重建、分割和几何计算）来回答多模态大语言模型（MLLM）仅凭像素无法解决的问题。然而，工具访问将瓶颈转移到了编排上：智能体必须决定调用哪些工具、以什么顺序调用以及如何解释其输出，而无状态智能体会在不同任务中重复同样的错误。我们探究一个冻结的工具使用智能体能否在不更新参数的情况下，从其自身的交互历史中改进编排能力。我们提出SpatialCraft，一个自演化框架，维护两种形式的外部知识：经验（Experiences），通过对比同一任务的不同 rollout 蒸馏出的条件-动作策略；以及技能（Skills），具有显式激活和终止条件的多步过程。三项设计将这两条流耦合起来。经验可以锚定到某个技能上，仅在该技能激活时出现，而不是每个任务出现一次。对比效用分数（Contrastive Utility Score）通过在同一个任务的多次 rollout 中随机保留或不保留每个经验来估计其效果，将其贡献与任务难度分离，并将具有持续正效用的经验整合到其所支持的技能中。在五个空间基准和四个骨干模型上，SpatialCraft 在所有20个设置中都取得了最佳准确率，表明一个冻结的智能体可以学会从自身经验中编排空间工具。

### How Does SAM-3 Adapt? Capacity, Update Geometry, and Domain Transfer

**中文标题：** SAM-3 如何适应？容量、更新几何与域迁移

**摘要：** Promptable segmentation foundation models are increasingly used in specialized domains, but it remains unclear how SAM-3 should be fine-tuned when adapting it beyond its original training distribution. We study this question by comparing five adaptation families --LoRA, HiRA, KRAdapter, QuanTA, and Full Fine-Tuning-- across five segmentation datasets spanning medical imaging, remote sensing, and dense material segmentation. Across these datasets, compact parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods often match or approach Full Fine-Tuning in DSC and IoU, suggesting that strong in-domain adaptation does not always require updating the full model. However, this performance cannot be explained by nominal adapter rank or trainable parameter count alone. To investigate why different methods perform similarly, we analyze their update spectra and their behavior under LoveDA Urban/Rural transfer. Our SVD analysis shows that Full Fine-Tuning updates are spectrally compressible. However, successful PEFT methods do not simply recover the same update directions; instead, they often concentrate changes in different components and subspaces. The domain-transfer study further shows that methods with similar in-domain performance can respond differently under distribution shift. Together, these results suggest that SAM-3 adaptation is often capacity-saturated in practice, while competitive fine-tuning arises through method-specific update paths rather than a single low-rank approximation to Full Fine-Tuning.

**摘要翻译：** 可提示分割基础模型正越来越多地用于专业领域，但当 SAM-3 被适配到其原始训练分布之外时，应如何对其进行微调仍不清楚。我们通过比较五种适配方法家族——LoRA、HiRA、KRAdapter、QuanTA 和全量微调——在涵盖医学影像、遥感和密集材料分割的五个分割数据集上来研究这一问题。在这些数据集上，紧凑的参数高效微调方法在 DSC 和 IoU 上往往匹配或接近全量微调，这表明强域内适配并不总是需要更新完整模型。然而，这一性能不能仅用名义适配器秩或可训练参数数量来解释。为研究不同方法为何表现相似，我们分析了它们的更新谱及其在 LoveDA 城市/乡村迁移下的行为。我们的 SVD 分析表明，全量微调的更新在谱上是可压缩的。然而，成功的 PEFT 方法并不只是恢复相同的更新方向；相反，它们往往将变化集中在不同的分量和子空间中。域迁移研究进一步表明，域内性能相似的方法在分布偏移下可能表现出不同响应。总之，这些结果表明，SAM-3 适配在实践中往往处于容量饱和状态，而具有竞争力的微调是通过方法特定的更新路径产生的，而不是对全量微调的单一低秩近似。
