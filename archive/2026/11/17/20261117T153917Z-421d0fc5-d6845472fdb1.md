## 论文条目

### Whole-Slice Misses Escape Round-Trip Filtering and Volumetric Dice in a Source-Free Flow-Matching Segmenter

**中文标题：** 整层漏检逃逸往返过滤与体积Dice：无源流匹配分割器中的问题

**摘要：** SymmAdapt, a source-free flow-matching segmentation pipeline, relies on two quality gates: a round-trip filter on pseudo-labels and volumetric Dice in evaluation. We audit both on 120 AMOS CT patients unseen in training and adaptation. The model often misses the spleen on whole slices, severely so in 30.0% of patients. The round-trip filter passes 83.31% of these lost slices, because its round trip reproduces the miss and the filter scores only organs present in the pseudo-label. Volumetric Dice depends on how much volume is missed, not where, and self-training leaves 96.5% of lost slices lost (96.6% on a second noise stream). A model-free repair, Chain-and-Fill, met all pre-registered endpoints on 119 further patients and could not be distinguished from SAM 2 propagation in severe-group gain, with 1/357 rather than 63/357 harmed patient-seed pairs. Reporting lost slices next to volumetric Dice makes the error visible at no cost.

**摘要翻译：** SymmAdapt是一个无源流匹配分割流程，依赖两个质量门控：对伪标签的往返过滤和评估中的体积Dice。我们在训练和自适应阶段均未见过的120名AMOS CT患者上审计了这两个门控。模型经常在整层上漏检脾脏，在30.0%的患者中尤为严重。往返过滤通过了这些漏检层中的83.31%，因为其往返过程重现了漏检，且过滤器仅对伪标签中存在的器官评分。体积Dice取决于漏检的体积量而非位置，自训练使96.5%的漏检层仍被漏检（在第二噪声流上为96.6%）。一种无模型修复方法Chain-and-Fill在另外119名患者上达到了所有预注册终点，在严重组增益上无法与SAM 2传播区分，受害患者-种子对为1/357而非63/357。在体积Dice旁报告漏检层可使该错误以零成本可见。
