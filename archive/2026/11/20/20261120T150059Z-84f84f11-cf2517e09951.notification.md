### A Unified Framework for Image Manipulation Localization Under Multi-Type Weak Supervision

**中文标题：** 多类型弱监督下图像篡改定位的统一框架

**摘要：** Existing weakly supervised image manipulation localization (IML) methods typically rely on annotation-specific training strategies, hindering the joint use of diverse annotations within a single model. This limits the scale of usable training data and constrains further progress. We propose UniIML, a teacher-student framework that maps diverse weak annotations into a unified representation to guide the Segment Anything Model (SAM) in generating multiple candidate masks. However, SAM is designed for general-purpose segmentation and has limited sensitivity to manipulation traces; its predicted quality scores alone may therefore fail to reliably identify the candidate that best delineates the manipulated region. We introduce Ordinal Safe Consensus (OSC), which first filters candidates using trusted weak-annotation constraints encoded in the unified representation. It then ranks the remaining candidates separately by consistency with weak annotations, SAM-predicted quality scores, and cross-view stability, aggregating these rankings for an initial selection. To assess how changes in the evaluation criteria affect candidate selection, OSC repeats ranking and selection while omitting one criterion at a time. The most frequently selected candidate is adopted as the final mask, and its relative selection frequency quantifies selection stability. We construct soft pseudo-labels from the selected candidate's predicted probabilities and estimate pixel-level supervision weights based on selection stability, pixel-level agreement among candidates, and predictive certainty. The student receives direct supervision in regions defined by weak annotations and learns from soft pseudo-labels elsewhere through reliability-weighted distillation, enabling unified training across weak annotation types. Experiments show that UniIML achieves superior average performance compared with state-of-the-art IML methods.

**摘要翻译：** 现有的弱监督图像篡改定位（IML）方法通常依赖于针对特定标注的训练策略，阻碍了在单一模型中联合使用多种标注。这限制了可用训练数据的规模，并制约了进一步进展。我们提出UniIML，一个教师-学生框架，将多种弱标注映射为统一表示，以引导Segment Anything Model（SAM）生成多个候选掩码。然而，SAM是为通用分割设计的，对篡改痕迹的敏感性有限；因此，仅凭其预测的质量分数可能无法可靠地识别出最能刻画篡改区域的候选。我们引入序数安全共识（OSC），它首先利用统一表示中编码的可信弱标注约束过滤候选。然后，它分别根据与弱标注的一致性、SAM预测的质量分数以及跨视图稳定性对剩余候选进行排序，并聚合这些排序以进行初步选择。为了评估评估标准的变化如何影响候选选择，OSC在每次省略一个标准的情况下重复排序和选择。被最频繁选择的候选被采纳为最终掩码，其相对选择频率量化了选择稳定性。我们从所选候选的预测概率构建软伪标签，并基于选择稳定性、候选间的像素级一致性以及预测确定性估计像素级监督权重。学生在弱标注定义的区域接受直接监督，并通过可靠性加权蒸馏从其他区域的软伪标签中学习，从而实现跨弱标注类型的统一训练。实验表明，与最先进的IML方法相比，UniIML取得了更优的平均性能。
