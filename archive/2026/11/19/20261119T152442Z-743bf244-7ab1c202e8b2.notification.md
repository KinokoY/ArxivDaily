### NativeSeg: Native Vision Language Segmentation of 3D Computed Tomography via Spatial Visual Tokens and Verified Local Zoom

**中文标题：** NativeSeg：通过空间视觉令牌与验证式局部缩放对三维计算机断层扫描进行原生视觉语言分割

**摘要：** Text prompted 3D computed tomography (CT) segmentation requires both full slice context and fine boundary precision across volumes where over $80\%$ of organ prompted slices lack the queried target. Existing medical vision language models (VLMs) are largely non native segmenters: they delegate pixel delineation outside the VLM by compressing reasoning into a 1D [SEG] vector for an external Segment Anything Model (SAM) decoder, quantizing masks into lossy discrete codebooks, or issuing box prompts to stateless external SAM tools without empty slice screening or spatial state memory. We introduce NativeSeg, a native vision language segmentation framework on Low Rank Adaptation (LoRA) adapted Qwen3.8-27B that performs dense mask decoding, volumetric triage, spatial error self diagnosis, and multiround local zoom directly over the VLM's 2D spatial visual tokens without external SAM decoders. NativeSeg decodes confidence maps from upper layer 2D spatial tokens fused with a five slice U-Net pathway, halts at zero zooms on $>91\%$ of standalone evaluations via a native presence gate (<pres>), localizes uncertain boundaries via a 20 cell multiscale error gate (<err>), and accepts zoomed updates only when predicted error decreases. Trained solely on adult CT, NativeSeg improves Round $0$ DSC by $+19.81$ and $+7.04$ points over discrete codebook and 1D [SEG] readouts on the identical backbone, reaches $86.81\%$ adult and $87.04\%$ zero shot pediatric DSC, and safely refines 2D nnU-Net ($89.52\%$ adult; $86.86\%$ pediatric), 3D SegVol ($86.81\%$), and 3D VoxTell ($89.85\%$).

**摘要翻译：** 文本提示的三维计算机断层扫描（CT）分割需要完整的切片上下文和精细的边界精度，而在体数据中，超过 $80\%$ 的器官提示切片缺少所查询的目标。现有医学视觉语言模型（VLM）大多不是原生分割器：它们将像素勾画委托给 VLM 之外，通过将推理压缩为一维 [SEG] 向量供外部 Segment Anything Model（SAM）解码器使用、将掩码量化为有损离散码本，或向无状态的外部 SAM 工具发出框提示，而缺乏空切片筛查或空间状态记忆。我们提出 NativeSeg，一个在经 Low Rank Adaptation（LoRA）适配的 Qwen3.8-27B 上的原生视觉语言分割框架，它直接在 VLM 的二维空间视觉令牌上执行稠密掩码解码、体数据分诊、空间误差自诊断和多轮局部缩放，无需外部 SAM 解码器。NativeSeg 从上层的二维空间令牌与五切片 U-Net 通路融合中解码置信图，通过原生存在门（<pres>）在超过 $91\%$ 的独立评估上以零缩放停止，通过 20 单元多尺度误差门（<err>）定位不确定边界，并且仅当预测误差下降时才接受缩放更新。仅在成人 CT 上训练，NativeSeg 在相同骨干上相较离散码本和一维 [SEG] 读出将 Round $0$ DSC 分别提升 $+19.81$ 和 $+7.04$ 点，达到 $86.81\%$ 成人 DSC 和 $87.04\%$ 零样本儿科 DSC，并安全地细化二维 nnU-Net（$89.52\%$ 成人；$86.86\%$ 儿科）、三维 SegVol（$86.81\%$）和三维 VoxTell（$89.85\%$）。
