### Fiberpose: state-of-the-art fiber morphometry and instance segmentation with a distance-transform neural network

**中文标题：** Fiberpose：基于距离变换神经网络的最先进纤维形态计量与实例分割

**摘要：** Analysis of fibrous structures in SEM images is a routine task in biomedical engineering and materials science. However, most available methods for fiber quantification still rely on classical image binarization followed by simple post-processing. These approaches often produce unreliable results in images with weak boundaries, heterogeneous contrast, and densely packed fibers. Here, we introduce Fiberpose, a framework that reformulates fiber analysis as direct regression of a smooth distance-transform (DT) field, together with a novel set of geometry-based algorithms specifically adapted to this representation. The predicted field directly encodes fiber geometry, while the accompanying algorithms enable instance reconstruction and measurement of diameter and orientation within a unified workflow. The framework is implemented as a standalone Python desktop application and a web tool https://fiberpose.org. On an independent dataset of fibrous SEM images, Fiberpose achieved state-of-the-art instance-segmentation accuracy in comparison with SAM2 and Cellpose-SAM, and more accurate diameter measurements than DiameterJ, Fibar and GIFT. The method remains effective in dense and weakly contrasted regions and shows qualitative generalization across microscopy modalities not represented in the training data. Fiberpose therefore provides an interpretable geometric representation and an automated pipeline for reproducible quantitative characterization of fibrous materials.

**摘要翻译：** SEM图像中纤维结构的分析是生物医学工程和材料科学的常规任务。然而，大多数可用的纤维量化方法仍依赖于经典图像二值化及简单后处理。这些方法在边界微弱、对比度不均和纤维密集的图像中往往产生不可靠的结果。在此，我们提出Fiberpose，一个将纤维分析重新表述为平滑距离变换（DT）场直接回归的框架，并配套一套专门适配该表示的新型基于几何的算法。预测场直接编码纤维几何，配套算法可在统一工作流中实现实例重建以及直径和方向测量。该框架实现为独立的Python桌面应用程序和网络工具https://fiberpose.org。在独立的纤维SEM图像数据集上，Fiberpose相比SAM2和Cellpose-SAM实现了最先进的实例分割精度，并且相比DiameterJ、Fibar和GIFT实现了更准确的直径测量。该方法在密集和弱对比区域仍然有效，并在训练数据未包含的显微模态上表现出定性泛化能力。因此，Fiberpose为纤维材料的可重复定量表征提供了可解释的几何表示和自动化流程。
